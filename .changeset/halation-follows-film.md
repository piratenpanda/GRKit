Halation has a Follow Film setting. While it is on and the Film Look names its film, the rim follows that film in place of Color: Orange for a film with a strong anti-halation layer, and a redder, one step stronger rim for one with a weak layer (Gold 200, UltraMax 400, Fujicolor C200, Superia 400, Kodachrome 64). Recipes and the XMP of a photo hold it.
